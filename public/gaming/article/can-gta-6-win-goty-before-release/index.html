Can GTA 6 Win Game of the Year Before Its Release?

A November 19 launch looked dangerously close to the cutoff, yet pre-launch review access landed Rockstar right on the December 10 ballot.

Ethan Brooks
Senior Gaming Editor

Quick Answer & Key Takeaway

Rockstar got pre-launch builds into jury hands early enough for GTA 6 to sweep eight nominations, including Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, and Best Performance for Manni L. Perez. Critics still point out a subtle catch: early review windows on massive open-world games can leave less time for long-tail reflection compared to titles released back in spring or summer.

Seeing Grand Theft Auto 6 headline the Game of the Year list on November 17, two full days before players could even launch the game on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, immediately sparked confusion across social media. How does a game get nominated before its street date, and can it actually win? The short answer to the second question is yes, and by the time the trophy is handed out on December 10, millions of people will have played it for three weeks.

The confusion stems from how The Game Awards calendar works. Every year, the global jury of gaming outlets submits nomination ballots ahead of the mid-November announcement, and the official eligibility cutoff allows games releasing within the late-November window to qualify so long as reviewers receive advance code in time to evaluate them properly.

Even so, final winner voting happens after launch, not before. Between November 19 and the Peacock Theater ceremony on December 10, both the international media jury (which carries 90 percent of the final vote) and fans casting public ballots (the remaining 10 percent) will have weeks of real-world experience in Leonida.

Tough competition on the 2026 ballot

Eligibility does not hand Rockstar a free trophy. This year's Game of the Year field is unusually fierce, pitting GTA 6 against 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and Ocarina of Time Remake. If GTA 6 delivers on its review acclaim once the full public gets its hands on Vice City, December 10 could easily belong to Rockstar, though nostalgia for Hyrule and love for Remedy's surreal sequel make it a genuine race.
