Lexie Shoaibi in GTA 6: Who is Mandy, house location, viral scene, and actress details

Everything players want to know about viral GTA 6 character Mandy, actress Lexie Shoaibi, her house location in Vice City, and the famous scene.

JK
Jordan Kim
Staff Writer, Gaming Desk
Nov 20, 2026 • 5 min read

Within hours of Grand Theft Auto 6 hitting consoles, clips of a glamorous, fast-talking Vice City socialite named Mandy took over social media. Search volume around actress Lexie Shoaibi, Mandy's mansion location, and her unhinged cutscene surged onto Google Trends. Here are direct, verified answers to every question surrounding GTA 6's breakout viral NPC.

Who is Mandy in GTA 6 and who is actress Lexie Shoaibi? (Mandy GTA 6 actress)

Mandy is a wealthy Leonida influencer and socialite who appears as a recurring stranger and world encounter in Vice City. British-Iranian actress and high-fashion model Lexie Shoaibi confirmed on social media that she provided the complete likeness, motion capture, and voice acting for the character.

Lexie Shoaibi is an accomplished actress and digital creator known for international editorial fashion campaigns and commercial voiceover work. Her sharp comedic timing, deadpan sarcasm, and expressive performance brought instant personality to Mandy, turning what could have been a standard background NPC into one of the most recognizable and beloved characters of launch week.

Where is Mandy's house in GTA 6? (Where is Mandy's house in GTA 6?)

Mandy's residence is a luxury waterfront estate located on Starfish Island, the prestigious gated island situated in the bay connecting Vice City Beach to the mainland financial district. To find the property:

1. Enter Starfish Island: Cross the main bridge leading onto Starfish Island from the eastern beach avenue.

2. Follow the Northern Circle: Drive past the central landscaped fountain and take the northern road loop toward the open bay.

3. Spot the Villa: Look for a grand Spanish-revival villa with a red terracotta roof, marble outdoor pillars, and a pearl-white Grotti sports convertible parked out front.

Players can walk onto the patio freely during daytime hours or enter through the side garden gate near the boat dock.

What is the viral GTA 6 Mandy scene? (GTA 6 Mandy scene / Mandy scene GTA 6)

The viral scene that captivated millions of viewers across TikTok, YouTube, and X takes place during a patio cocktail party at Mandy's mansion. When Jason or Lucia approaches the pool deck, Mandy immediately mistakes them for hired private caterers, launching into a hilariously self-absorbed monologue about synthetic cryptocurrency yachts, pet cougars, and Vice City socialite rivalries.

What made the moment truly iconic is what happens if chaos erupts. If the player discharges a firearm or triggers police stars near the patio, typical NPCs flee in terror. Mandy, however, whips out her gold smartphone, starts live-streaming the shootout to her followers, and delivers deadpan color commentary while calmly sipping champagne and ducking behind poolside deck chairs.

How do you trigger the Mandy encounter in Vice City?

To experience the Mandy encounter firsthand, you must first complete the Chapter 1 storyline mission "Running the Numbers," which lifts travel restrictions across Vice City waterways. Once Chapter 1 is finished, visit Starfish Island between 2:00 PM and 8:00 PM in-game time.

An orange stranger event blip will appear near the northern villa's poolside cabana. Interacting with Mandy unlocks her unique dialogue sequence, introduces an optional side mission to recover stolen designer jewelry, and adds her contact card to your in-game smartphone for occasional party invites.

Why is the GTA 6 community obsessed with Mandy?

Gamers fell in love with Mandy because she represents Rockstar Games' satire of modern Miami influencer culture at its sharpest. Between Lexie Shoaibi's energetic voice performance, detailed facial animations, and fearless reaction to open-world gunfights, Mandy encapsulates the wild, unpredictable energy that defines Grand Theft Auto 6.
