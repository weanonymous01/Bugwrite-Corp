How long is GTA 6 story mode? Campaign hours, chapter structure, and 100 percent completion estimates

Expect roughly 50 hours for a focused run across five chapters, 80 hours with major side paths, and near 200 hours to see everything.

EB
Ethan Brooks
Senior Gaming Editor
Nov 18, 2026 • 2 min read
Quick Answer & Key Takeaway

Very few players treat a Rockstar open world as a straight line from mission marker to mission marker, and doing the extra legwork adds significant mileage. Internal developer playthroughs, including a full run cited from Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson that mixed main story beats with deeper exploration and optional side goals carrying narrative consequences, landed closer to the 80-hour mark.

Anyone booking time off work for Grand Theft Auto VI should prepare for a long stay in Leonida. Pre-launch campaign details and developer playthrough estimates indicate that a focused run through the main story takes roughly 50 hours, placing GTA 6 much closer in scale to Red Dead Redemption 2 than to the brisker 30-hour main storyline of Grand Theft Auto V.

According to pre-release reports, the central narrative spans nearly 100 missions organized across five distinct chapters, echoing the structured pacing Rockstar used to build momentum throughout Arthur Morgan's journey in 2018.

Story plus side objectives pushes closer to 80 hours

That middle tier likely represents what most everyday players will experience over their first few weeks: following Jason and Lucia's main arc while peeling off to take on neighborhood heists, character side stories, street races, and dynamic encounters scattered between Vice City and the Leonida Keys.

The 200-hour climb for completionists

For trophy hunters chasing a 100 percent completion checklist or PlayStation Platinum Trophy, the commitment stretches dramatically further. Estimates put full completion around 200 hours once collectibles, mini-games, side challenges, and deep wildlife cataloging are factored in, including tracking down and scanning all 170 animal species inhabiting Leonida's wetlands, coasts, and backroads.

Whether you plan to race straight to the credits to dodge spoilers or spend months exploring every corner of the map, Rockstar's latest sandbox is built to dominate player calendars well into 2027.
