GTA 6 endings guide: How many story conclusions exist and how choices shape the finale

Rockstar's campaign features three distinct narrative conclusions shaped by trust meters, final heist decisions, and relationship loyalty.

SP
Samira Patel
Senior Gaming Editor
Nov 20, 2026 • 6 min read

As players push through the gripping narrative of Grand Theft Auto 6, search volume around the campaign's endings has skyrocketed. With dual protagonists bound by a modern Bonnie and Clyde dynamic, the central question for fans is whether the story forces a tragic betrayal or allows both characters to walk away into the sunset. GTA 6 features three primary ending paths, determined by key relationship choices and a final tactical decision presented during the Port Gellhorn federal standoff.

Unlike GTA 5's straightforward menu choice at the end of the campaign, GTA 6 weaves its conclusions into a dynamic trust mechanic that tracks decisions made throughout the sixty-hour storyline. How you treat your partner during armed heists, safehouse discussions, and crew disputes directly colors the emotional tone of the climax.

The Trust System and how it builds toward the finale

Throughout the story, players encounter critical prompts where Jason and Lucia can either prioritize shared survival or selfish gain. Splitting robbery payouts equitably, offering covering fire during intense police pursuits, and being honest during narrative dialogues builds mutual loyalty. Conversely, stashing extra contraband for personal profit or cutting side deals with rival syndicates chips away at that bond.

When the final chapter arrives, this underlying trust score sets the baseline for the finale. A high trust rating unlocks the most cooperative dialogue options, while strained trust introduces suspicion and hesitation when the federal dragnet closes in.

Ending A: The Clean Break (Solidarity Path)

In the most popular and harmonious narrative path, Jason and Lucia choose complete solidarity against both federal agents and rival Leonida cartels. In this finale, players switch dynamically between both characters as they execute an escape plan through the Port Gellhorn industrial canals.

Jason provides heavy rifle support from the harbor crane while Lucia navigates a modified offshore speedboat through a naval blockade. Both protagonists survive the encounter, successfully laundering their overseas accounts and vanishing into South America. Post-game free roam remains fully intact, with both characters playable and unique partner interactions unlocked across Leonida safehouses.

Ending B and Ending C: Individual sacrifices

If trust between the pair has deteriorated, or if the player opts for an alternate split during the terminal warehouse standoff, the story branches into two poignant sacrifice outcomes. In Ending B, Jason takes the fall, deliberately drawing federal task force attention to allow Lucia to slip through the cordon with the master ledger. In Ending C, Lucia stays behind to detonate harbor fuel tanks, ensuring Jason's clean getaway into northern Leonida.

Each alternate route triggers distinct epilogue cinematics, unique radio news broadcasts detailing the aftermath, and heartfelt voicemail recordings on the surviving partner's phone. While both alternate endings are emotionally powerful, post-game free roam for the sacrificed character is replaced with a flashback memory mechanic.

Can you replay missions to see every ending?

Rockstar makes experiencing all three conclusions straightforward without requiring a full sixty-hour replay. Once the credits finish rolling, players can access the Chapter 5 finale directly through the Pause menu's Replay Mission tab. You can test alternate dialogue prompts and see each cinematographic conclusion while keeping your primary completed save file safe.
