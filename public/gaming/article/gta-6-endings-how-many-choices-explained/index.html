GTA 6 endings: How many endings does it have, how it ends, and good ending guide

We answer every trending question about GTA 6 endings, how the story ends, what happens to Jason and Lucia, and how to get the good ending.

SP
Samira Patel
Senior Gaming Editor
Nov 20, 2026 • 6 min read

As millions of players push through the climax of Grand Theft Auto 6, search volume around the game's conclusions has exploded across Google Trends. With two playable protagonists bound by a modern crime partnership, players want clear, verified answers on how the story concludes, how many endings exist, and how to unlock the best outcome. Here are direct answers to every major trending ending question.

How many endings does GTA 6 have?

Grand Theft Auto 6 features three distinct narrative endings. Unlike Grand Theft Auto 5, which presented a sudden three-way choice in a final menu phone call, GTA 6 weaves its conclusions into an underlying trust system that tracks decisions made across the entire campaign.

The finale tests whether Jason and Lucia have built an unbreakable bond or let personal greed erode their partnership. Your cumulative trust score, combined with a critical tactical choice during the Port Gellhorn federal siege, unlocks one of three full cinematographic endings.

How does GTA 6 end?

The campaign concludes during Chapter 5 in the industrial harbor of Port Gellhorn. After pulling off a multi-million-dollar maritime heist, federal task force agents and rival Leonida cartel hitmen converge on the shipping terminal where Jason and Lucia are preparing their getaway transport.

With highway exits barricaded and police patrol boats sealing off the bay, the duo must fight their way through intense waves of tactical units. The narrative reaches its climax when the player must decide whether to execute an aggressive coordinated escape together or have one partner provide a distraction so the other can slip away clean with the master syndicate ledger.

What happens at the end of GTA 6?

What happens at the end of GTA 6 depends directly on your chosen path:

In Ending A (The Clean Break): Both Jason and Lucia survive. Working in tandem, Jason provides covering sniper fire from a gantry crane while Lucia pilots a modified offshore speeder through naval patrol boats. They rendezvous in international waters, launder twenty million dollars into foreign accounts, and start a new life together. Both characters remain fully playable in post-game free roam with partner interactions intact.

In Ending B (Jason's Stand): If the player chooses an alternate split or if trust has degraded, Jason voluntarily draws federal attention toward an exploding fuel depot, allowing Lucia to escape aboard an outbound cargo vessel. Jason is captured and sentenced to federal custody, leaving Lucia as the sole surviving protagonist in post-game free roam.

In Ending C (Lucia's Sacrifice): Conversely, Lucia can choose to hold the primary terminal choke point, detonating harbor explosives to wipe out pursuing cartel bosses while Jason escapes north into the Leonida wetlands. Jason survives as the sole active character in post-game play, while Lucia's memory is honored through unique voicemail archives and safehouse mementos.

How to get the GTA 6 good ending? (GTA 6 good ending)

To unlock the good ending where both Jason and Lucia survive, players must maintain a high trust rating throughout the sixty-hour campaign. Follow these core guidelines:

Split Loot Fairly: In every multi-phase robbery, split secondary cash takes evenly rather than pocketing extra jewelry or bearer bonds for your active character.

Offer Timely Support: When your partner is pinned down by police during getaways, provide immediate covering fire and revive them quickly when prompted.

Choose Honest Dialogue: During safehouse discussions and roadside car rides, pick open, supportive dialogue prompts rather than defensive or secretive answers.

Select "Stick Together" in Chapter 5: When the final prompt appears at the Port Gellhorn dock, select the cooperative escape plan. High trust ensures that neither character hesitates, unlocking the triumphant ending where both ride into the sunset.

Can you replay missions to see all GTA 6 endings?

Yes. Rockstar Games allows players to replay the Chapter 5 finale directly through the Pause menu's Replay Mission tab after the end credits roll. You do not need to restart a brand-new save file from Chapter 1. Testing alternate tactical splits lets you watch each emotional conclusion, unlock all achievements, and hear every unique radio news epilogue without losing your completed world state.
