GTA 6 Download Size: How Much Storage Will You Need?

With preload already live and the install sitting around 212 GB, base PS5 and 512 GB Xbox Series S owners will need to do serious spring cleaning.

JK
Jordan Kim
Games & Hardware Reporter
Nov 16, 2026 • 3 min read

If you plan to play Grand Theft Auto 6 at midnight on November 19, start clearing your console drive now. Preload opened on November 10 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, revealing a hefty install footprint of roughly 212 GB. Even by late-generation standards, Rockstar's return to Vice City immediately ranks among the largest console downloads ever released.

Because modern consoles require extra temporary overhead during patching and decompression, you should aim to leave at least 230 GB to 250 GB free before kicking off the download. Cutting it right to the edge risks triggering an insufficient space warning the moment a day-one patch arrives.

Why PS5 and Xbox Series S owners face the tightest squeeze

The 212 GB footprint hits different console configurations unevenly. On an original launch PS5 with 667 GB of usable internal storage, GTA 6 consumes nearly a third of the entire drive on its own. Owners of the 512 GB Xbox Series S have it even tighter: with roughly 364 GB actually usable after operating system files, installing Leonida leaves little room for more than one or two medium-sized games alongside it.

Remember that GTA 6 is a current-generation native title, which means it cannot run directly from a standard USB external hard drive. You can store inactive games on a cheap USB drive to free up room, then move them back later, or play GTA 6 directly from a compatible PCIe Gen 4 NVMe SSD on PS5 or an official Storage Expansion Card on Xbox.

How to prepare before unlock

Digital buyers who have not started preloading should queue the download overnight, as a 212 GB transfer takes nearly five hours even on a steady 100 Mbps connection. Physical disc buyers should also expect substantial data copying plus a launch update before stepping onto Ocean Beach, making an early storage audit well worth ten minutes tonight.
