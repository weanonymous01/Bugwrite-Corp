About Bugwrite

Bugwrite is an independent digital publication covering technology, video games, cybersecurity, and the business of artificial intelligence. Published daily at bugwrite.com, our goal is simple: explain complex technical events clearly, verify every claim against primary sources, and respect the reader's time.

Our Editorial Standards

Every article on Bugwrite undergoes fact verification before publication. For service outages, we cross-reference official status dashboards and vendor disclosures rather than repeating unverified social media rumors. For cybersecurity reporting, we verify CVE advisories, patch releases, and federal law enforcement bulletins directly. For gaming and hardware coverage, we clearly separate confirmed publisher announcements from industry rumors.

Our Newsroom Desks

Bugwrite operates four core desks. Our Gaming desk, led by Samira Patel and Jordan Kim, covers major console and PC launches, patch notes, subscription catalogs, and hardware markets. Our Big Tech and Security desks, led by Elena Rostova, Marcus Vance, and Priya Nair, report on cloud reliability, software vulnerabilities, operating systems, and consumer privacy. Our Business desk, led by David Lindholm, tracks semiconductor markets, AI infrastructure spending, and public offerings.

Corrections Policy

Accuracy comes first. If you spot a factual error or outdated technical detail in any Bugwrite article, please reach out through our Contact page so our editors can review and update the story promptly.
