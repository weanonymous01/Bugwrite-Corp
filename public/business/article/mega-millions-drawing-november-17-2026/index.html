Mega Millions drawing November 17, 2026: Winning numbers and why the jackpot climbed to $629 million

No ticket matched all six numbers for Tuesday's $600 million prize, setting up a $629 million drawing on Friday, November 20.

Nov 18, 2026 • 2 min read

The Mega Millions jackpot rolled over once again after Tuesday night's drawing on November 17, 2026 produced no grand-prize winner. Heading into the draw, the estimated annuity prize stood at $600 million, drawing heavy ticket sales across participating U.S. states.

The official winning white balls drawn on Tuesday, November 17, 2026 were 03, 10, 19, 28, and 33, with the gold Mega Ball 21. Because no single ticket matched all six numbers, the jackpot climbs again ahead of the weekend.

What is at stake for Friday, November 20

Lottery officials updated the estimated jackpot for the next Mega Millions drawing on Friday, November 20, 2026 to $629 million. Players who prefer a lump-sum payout over the 30-year annuity schedule are looking at an estimated cash option of $258.8 million before federal and applicable state taxes.

How to check secondary prizes from Tuesday night

Even without a jackpot winner, tickets that matched fewer numbers can still carry meaningful lower-tier payouts depending on whether players matched the Mega Ball or three to five white balls. Anyone holding a ticket from the November 17 drawing should double-check their numbers against their official state lottery website or scan tickets at an authorized retailer, since claim windows and prize tiers vary slightly by jurisdiction.
